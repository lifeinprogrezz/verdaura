N° 01   The market

One price zone quietly kills new brands.

Demand per product by price band. It does not fall smoothly. At $120 to $139 it collapses, the point where a shopper stops trusting the price.

The opening

The natural luxury tier is nearly empty.

Eau de Toilette moves the volume. The richest, most natural concentration is barely stocked, and it holds the highest price. That gap is the brand.

N° 04   The product

Ombrage, the first release.

French for shade. A higher concentration natural composition, priced to be earned. Shortlist and winner:

Rose, oud and amber are the three luxury notes the Task 1 data shows carry real demand, and the natural niche tier is under supplied. A higher concentration base gives the longevity that justifies a committed luxury price.

N° 05   The launch

Earned, not advertised.

Earned, not advertised. Verdaura is the natural luxury you discover, not the one that follows you around the internet.

The film

Ombrage, a sixty second film. Dawn in a botanical garden, hands harvesting rose, a resin of oud warming over a low flame, the flacon taking shape, one person in quiet clothes moving through green shade. No celebrity, no wall of logos. Natural light, slow, cinematic.

Verdaura. Living botanical perfumery.

It sells the two things the data says this buyer cannot find, real natural craft and understated luxury, and it shows sustainability rather than claiming it.

The campaign, one message per buyer:

The Considered Connoisseur

Editorial and curated boutiques, a seeded sample program, word of mouth

The one nobody can place. Higher concentration, traceable materials, priced to be earned.

The Conscious Luxe Professional

Instagram and clean beauty partnerships, sustainability storytelling

Know what is in it and who made it. Refillable, biodegradable, natural.

The Aspirational Explorer

Short form video and fragrance communities, discovery sets

Start with a sample. If it becomes your scent, the bottle is waiting.

The launch sequence:

1SeedSample the connoisseurs and a few trusted creators first. Scarcity, not a blast.
2DiscoverShip discovery sets and decants so the explorer can try before the bottle.
3DirectOpen the website with the refill program built in, then a short list of curated boutiques.
4RetainHalf price refills and one hero, so loyalty compounds instead of chasing flankers.

Watch sell through of the launch batch, sample to bottle conversion, and the refill rate, not reach.
